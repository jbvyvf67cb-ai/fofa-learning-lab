Adaptive Spelling Bee

The word is read aloud; you type it. Get it right and words climb harder; miss one and it eases off and brings that word back later. Practise as long as you like.
How it works

Meets you where you are

Every word is rated for difficulty and for how tricky it is to spell — silent letters, double letters, ie/ei, tricky endings. The lab tracks your level and picks the next word from a bank of 30,000+ words. No grades, no ceiling.

Tip: it works best with sound on — the word is spoken aloud. If audio isn't available it switches to flash mode (see the word for a moment, then spell it from memory).
