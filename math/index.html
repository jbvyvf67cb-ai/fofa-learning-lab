Math

The plan for math is the same as everywhere else in the lab: make the idea something you can move, stretch, and rearrange until it clicks — number lines you can drag, fractions you can cut, shapes you can fold. Here's the arc we're building toward.

The learning arc
  1. 1 Number sense — count, compare, and place numbers on a line you can drag and zoom.
  2. 2 Operations — add, subtract, multiply, and divide as things happening to groups you can see.
  3. 3 Fractions & decimals — cut wholes into parts and watch the same amount wear different names.
  4. 4 Ratios & proportion — scale recipes and maps and feel quantities move together.
  5. 5 Geometry & measurement — build, fold, and measure shapes; discover why the formulas are true.
  6. 6 Into algebra — balance both sides of an equation like a scale and solve for the unknown.
