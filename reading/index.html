Reading

Reading in the lab means doing something with the words — highlighting who did what, predicting what comes next, mapping a story's shape — and then showing what you understood. Open-ended answers can be graded with feedback through Home Assistant. Here's the arc.

The learning arc
  1. 1 Decoding & phonics — blend sounds into words and see spelling patterns light up.
  2. 2 Fluency — read passages with pacing and expression, and track it over time.
  3. 3 Vocabulary — meet new words in context, from roots and prefixes you can assemble.
  4. 4 Comprehension — mark the main idea, evidence, and inferences right on the text.
  5. 5 Story structure — map characters, setting, and the arc of a plot.
  6. 6 Response & analysis — write about what you read; get feedback graded by Claude via Home Assistant.
