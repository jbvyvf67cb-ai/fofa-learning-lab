¶ Writing Lab
Language arts, made interactive

Learn to write by taking sentences apart.

Start with the smallest unit of writing — the sentence — and work outward: parts of speech, how they lock into subjects and predicates, phrases and clauses, sentence types, and on into style. Click any word to see its job, drag it somewhere new, and watch the meaning move.

Experiences

We’re starting at the very beginning: Unit 1 — the parts of speech, the building blocks every later lesson is made of. Meet the whole sentence first, learn each kind of word and how to spot it, then put it to the test.
