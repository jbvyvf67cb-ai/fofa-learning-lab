Unit 3 · Phrases & clauses

Phrase Painter

A phrase is a group of words that acts as one unit inside a sentence. Toggle a phrase type on to paint colored brackets under the words that belong to it. Phrases can overlap and nest — a noun phrase can contain a prepositional phrase inside it — so each active span gets its own row underneath the sentence.

Turn on a phrase type above, then click or hover a colored bracket to read what it does.

Colors and sentences come from the shared dataset in assets/data/grammar.js, so they mean the same thing in every module.  ·  Back to Writing Lab
