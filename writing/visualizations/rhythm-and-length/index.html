Rhythm & Length

Every sentence has a length, and a paragraph's lengths make a rhythm. Line them up as bars and the pattern jumps out: same-length sentences read flat, while a mix of short and long ones reads with punch and flow. Toggle the two paragraphs below to feel the difference.

Why it works: short sentences punch — they land hard and give the reader a breath. Long sentences flow — they carry momentum and connect ideas. A paragraph that mixes both has rhythm; a paragraph where every sentence is the same length reads like a metronome, and metronomes get monotonous fast.
