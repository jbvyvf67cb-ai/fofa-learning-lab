The big idea

The spot temperature = bulk baseline (set by how the outer surface sheds heat, over time) + local rise (set by how fast the lattice conducts heat away from the spot). Flip the Boundary control: a heat-sunk diamond can only be cut by a huge local rise (~40 W absorbed) and time doesn't help; an isolated diamond slowly preheats until even a weak beam cuts it — there, time is everything.

Model & assumptions

CW model with constant k (real k falls ~1/T, so hot-spot temps are underestimated); spherical source with a ≈ w₀; lumped-capacitance bulk; graphitization treated as a hard threshold (really a rate); absorbed fraction is a stand-in for uncertain nonlinear/defect absorption. Pulsed "cold ablation", wavelength, oxidation feedback and the moving cut are omitted.

Physics per ideas/laser-on-diamond.md: ΔTloc = Pabs/(4πk)(1/a − 1/b); bulk C·dT/dt = Pabs − hA(T−T∞) − εσA(T⁴−T∞⁴).
