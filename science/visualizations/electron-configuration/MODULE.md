# M3 · Electron Configuration & Shells — module content

**Explore:** the Electron Configuration & Shells viz (this folder).

**Owns:** electron shells / energy levels; orbitals & spin; **valence electrons**; the aufbau
(filling) order and its exceptions. (Valence is applied later in M4 groups and M5 bonding.)

## Planned lesson cards
1. **Energy levels (shells).** Electrons live in shells around the nucleus; inner shells fill
   first. Shell capacities (2, 8, 18…). Number of shells ≈ the element's period.
2. **Orbitals & spin.** Shells split into subshells (s, p, d, f); each orbital holds 2
   electrons with opposite spin (Pauli); electrons spread out singly first (Hund's rule).
3. **The aufbau order.** Electrons fill lowest energy first (1s, 2s, 2p, 3s…). Where the order
   surprises you (4s before 3d).
4. **Valence electrons.** The outer-shell electrons — the ones that drive chemistry. How to
   read the count. *(Hands off to M4 groups and M5 bonding.)*
5. **Exceptions.** A few elements (Cr, Cu…) break the simple rule because half/full subshells
   are extra stable.

## Try-it prompts
- Step through filling one electron at a time; watch shells build up.
- Find where the aufbau order jumps (4s before 3d).
- Compare a group-1 vs group-17 atom's valence count.

## Quiz seeds
- (mc) How many electrons fit in the first shell? _A: 2._
- (mc) Valence electrons are the electrons in the → innermost / **outermost** shell.
- (numeric) How many valence electrons does oxygen have? _A: 6._
- (build) Fill an atom to a full outer shell (noble-gas configuration).
