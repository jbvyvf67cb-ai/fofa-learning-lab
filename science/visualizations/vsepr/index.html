The VSEPR idea

All electron groups around a central atom — bonds and lone pairs — push as far apart as possible. That repulsion fixes the geometry. The shape name counts only the atoms you can see, but the lone pairs still bend things. And the shape decides polarity: if the bond dipoles cancel by symmetry, the molecule is nonpolar even with polar bonds (that's why CO₂ is nonpolar but H₂O is polar).
