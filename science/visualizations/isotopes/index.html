Why this matters

This weighted average is the atomic mass on the periodic table, and it's exactly the number you'll multiply by in stoichiometry to get molar mass (grams per mole). Non-whole masses come straight from isotope mixing.

Standard atomic weights for comparison come from the shared dataset (assets/data/elements.js). Isotope masses and natural abundances are curated per element.
