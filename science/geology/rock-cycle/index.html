The Rock Cycle

Rocks aren't forever. Over millions of years every rock gets turned into a different one, again and again.

1 Three families of rock

Every rock belongs to one of three families, named for how it was made:

Igneous rocks form when melted rock (magma or lava) cools and hardens. Cool it slowly underground and the crystals have time to grow big (granite). Cool it fast at the surface and the crystals stay tiny (basalt).

Sedimentary rocks form when bits of other rocks (sand, mud, shells) pile up in layers, get buried, and get glued together. That's where most fossils are found.

Metamorphic rocks are rocks that got changed by heat and squeezing deep underground, without melting. Limestone becomes marble; shale becomes slate.

The rock cycle is how one turns into another. Next you get to run the machine.
