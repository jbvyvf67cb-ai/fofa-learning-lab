Plate Tectonics

The ground under you is moving right now, about as fast as your fingernails grow.

1 A cracked eggshell

Earth's outer shell (the crust plus the stiff top of the mantle) is cracked into about 15 big pieces called plates. They ride on the hot mantle underneath, which slowly flows (remember lesson 1?). Plates move about 2 to 10 centimeters a year.

There are two kinds of plate. Oceanic plates are thin and heavy (made of basalt) and sit low, under the oceans. Continental plates are thick and light (lots of granite) and float high, so they make the land. Almost all the exciting geology happens where two plates meet, at a plate boundary.
