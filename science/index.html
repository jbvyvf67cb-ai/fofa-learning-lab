Build intuition, then optimize it.

Every visualization here is interactive and hands-on. You won't just read that carbon has six protons — you'll build the atom, turn it into an ion, make an isotope, and watch the numbers (charge, mass, shells) respond. The goal is to learn how systems work well enough to change them.

Your learning path

🔒 Lessons unlock in order — finish a module's quiz (80%+) to open the next one.

01

Atomic Structure — Element Explorer

Build any atom from protons, neutrons, and electrons. See the element, isotope, ion, charge, and electron shells update live — and whether what you built occurs in nature, only in the lab, in theory, or not at all.

 02

Isotopes & Average Atomic Mass

Mix an element's isotopes and watch the weighted-average mass move. See why chlorine is 35.45 — and meet the number that becomes molar mass in stoichiometry.

 03

Electron Configuration & Shells

Fill orbitals one electron at a time (s/p/d/f, spin, Hund's rule), watch shells and valence emerge, and meet the elements that break the aufbau rule.

 04

Periodic Table Explorer

All 118 elements. Colour by a trend, highlight a row, period, or group, and watch the shared property light up — radius, electronegativity, ionization energy.

 05

Chemical Bonding ★

Pick two atoms; predict ionic / covalent / metallic from electronegativity, then watch electrons transfer or share. Covalent focus: single/double/triple bonds, octets, polarity, and a molecule gallery.

 06

VSEPR Molecular Geometry ★

Set bonds and lone pairs, drag to rotate the 3D shape, and see how geometry decides polarity — why CO₂ is nonpolar but H₂O isn't.
